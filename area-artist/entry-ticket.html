A warm-up for the start of class, before students play or start the worksheet. It reviews rows, columns and skip counting using things students see every day, then asks them to think ahead to multiplying for area. Two tickets print on each page; cut along the dashed line. The answer key prints on its own page after it.

Answer Key: Entry Ticket

Rows and Rows
  1. 12 eggs
  2. 12 squares
  3. 12, 15; 15, 20
  4. 20. Skip count 5, 10, 15, 20, or multiply 4 × 5. That's today's lesson: area = rows × columns.

Quick read: students who count every egg one at a time are ready to learn skip counting by rows. Use #4 to open the lesson, then let students paint rectangles in Area Artist.
