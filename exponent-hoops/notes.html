Visual Notes: Exponent Rules with Variables

Grade 8, Algebra 1 · CCSS 8.EE.A.1 · Pairs with the Exponent Hoops game
Name

The big idea: x⁵ means x · x · x · x · x: five x's multiplied. The exponent rules are just counting x's. Multiply and the x's pile up (add). Divide and they cancel (subtract). Raise a power to a power and you copy the group (multiply).

Key vocabulary

Base

x5

The number or variable being multiplied: x.

Exponent

x5

How many times the base is a factor: 5.

Power

x³ = x·x·x

A base with an exponent.

Product of powers

x²·x³ = (x·x)(x·x·x) = x⁵

Same base, multiply: add exponents.

Quotient of powers

x⁵ ÷ x² = x·x·x·x·x⁄x·x = x³

Same base, divide: subtract exponents.

Power of a power

(x²)³ = x²·x²·x² = x⁶

Multiply the exponents.

Zero exponent

x⁰ = 1

x³ ÷ x³ = 1, and also x⁰. Any nonzero base to the 0 is 1.

The rules at a glance (same base only)

xa · xb = xa+bMultiply → add
y⁴ · y² = y⁶
xa ÷ xb = xa−bDivide → subtract
y⁷ ÷ y³ = y⁴
(xa)b = xabPower of a power → multiply
(y³)² = y⁶

Solved Step by Step: Shots at the Hoop

Visual Notes: Exponent Rules with Variables, page 2
m⁴ · m³

1. Multiply

  1. Same base? Yes, m. Multiplying, so add: 4 + 3 = 7
  2. m⁷
✓ Count: (m·m·m·m)(m·m·m) is seven m's
k⁹ ÷ k⁴

2. Divide

  1. Same base? Yes, k. Dividing, so subtract: 9 − 4 = 5
  2. k⁵
✓ Four k's cancel from top and bottom, five are left
(p³)⁴

3. Power of a power

  1. Four groups of p³: p³ · p³ · p³ · p³
  2. Multiply: 3 × 4 = 12, so p¹²
✓ Not p⁷: that would be adding
b² · b³ ÷ b⁷

4. Put the rules together

  1. Multiply first: b² · b³ = b⁵
  2. Then divide: b⁵ ÷ b⁷ = b⁻²
  3. Positive exponent: b⁻² = 1⁄b²
✓ Five b's on top, seven on the bottom: two are left below
Watch out
  • x² means x · x, not 2x. Test with x = 3: 9 is not 6.
  • Add exponents when you multiply powers; multiply exponents for a power of a power.
  • The rules need the same base: x² · y³ stays as it is.
