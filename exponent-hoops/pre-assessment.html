What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: PowersThe rules with letters only make sense once numbers make sense.Start with Exponent Racer, which teaches the same rules with numbers.
B: Variablesx⁴ is just x · x · x · x. Students who see that can count factors to check every rule.Write out every power the long way for the first lesson.
C: Equivalent expressions#8 is the classic x² = 2x mistake; #10 is why x² · y³ can't become one power.Test expressions with a number, as in #8, whenever students are unsure.

Reading the results: students strong in Parts A and B are ready to start the rules right away. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
