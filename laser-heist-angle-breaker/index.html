Laser Heist: Angle Breaker

A spy game about angle relationships. In each room, type the missing angle to aim a laser at a security panel: the laser fires at exactly the angle you type. Every panel you hit switches off part of the room's security (the cameras, the guards' radios, or the locked shortcut doors). Then sneak past the guards to the exit, collecting diamonds. Controls: type a number and press Enter to fire; arrow keys or WASD to move; 1 for a smoke bomb, 2 for a decoy; Escape opens the menu.

Applies to: Vaults 4–5

Quick Teacher Tips

Key vocabulary

  • complementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 90°
  • supplementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 180°
  • vertical angles: the angles across from each other where two lines cross; they are equal
  • parallel lines: lines in the same plane that never meet
  • transversal: a line that crosses two or more other lines

Tips for using the game

  • One floor per relationship: Floor 1 complementary, 2 supplementary, 3 vertical, 4 corresponding and alternate interior, 5 co-interior. Floor 6, the Algebra Vault, is all small equations like x + 3 (7.G.B.5).
  • The answer aims the laser: the laser fires at exactly the angle a student types, so a wrong answer visibly misses, and the steps to solve it are shown.
  • The math changes the sneak: each missed panel adds trouble (an extra camera, sharper guards, darkness, tripwires, more guards and an alarm clock). Students can redo missed questions any time with the R key, three catches after a miss sends them back to the questions, and 0 of 3 right means redoing all three before the sneak.
  • Stars measure the math: one star for each panel answered right on the first try.
  • Practice drills any mix of relationships with no guards or timer.
