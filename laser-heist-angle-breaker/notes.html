Visual Notes: Angle Relationships

Grades 7–8 · CCSS 7.G.B.5, 8.G.A.5 · Pairs with the Laser Heist: Angle Breaker game
Name

The big idea: when lines cross, their angles come in pairs that are either equal or add up to 90° or 180°. In the Laser Heist, you turn the laser by the angle you find. Spot the pair, decide “equal” or “adds up”, and solve.

Key vocabulary (the colors show the angles each word is about)

Complementary

Two angles that add to 90°.

Supplementary

Two angles that add to 180°.

Vertical angles

Across from each other where two lines cross. Equal.

Parallel lines

Lines in the same plane that never meet; they stay the same distance apart.

Transversal

A line that crosses two or more lines (blue).

Corresponding

Same spot at each crossing. Equal.

Alternate interior

Between the lines, opposite sides. Equal.

Co-interior

Between the lines, same side. Add to 180°.

Which rule do I use? (equal, or add up?)

A little box?Add to 90°
A straight line?Add to 180°
An X?Across: equal
A Z?Alternate interior: equal
An F?Corresponding: equal
A C or U?Co-interior: add to 180°
Shortcut for parallel lines: at two parallel lines cut by a transversal there are only two sizes of angle, a small one and a big one, and small + big = 180°. Every small angle is equal to every other small angle.

Solved Step by Step: Laser Puzzles

Visual Notes: Angle Relationships, page 2

1. Two beams cross

  1. x is across from 55°: vertical angles are equal, so x = 55°.
  2. y is next to 55° on a straight line: they add to 180°.
  3. y = 180 − 55 = 125°
✓ Check: 55 + 125 = 180

2. A beam across two parallel walls

∠2 = 72°. Find ∠6 and ∠8.

  1. ∠2 and ∠6 are corresponding (an F): equal, so ∠6 = 72°.
  2. ∠8 is a big angle (the small ones are 72°): ∠8 = 180 − 72 = 108°.
✓ Check: ∠6 + ∠8 make a straight line: 72 + 108 = 180

3. Co-interior angles with x

∠4 = (2x + 10)° and ∠6 = (x + 20)°.

  1. Between the lines, same side (a C): they add to 180°.
  2. (2x + 10) + (x + 20) = 180, so 3x + 30 = 180
  3. 3x = 150, x = 50. ∠4 = 110°, ∠6 = 70°
✓ Check: 110 + 70 = 180

4. Challenge: complementary with x

  1. The box means they add to 90°: x + (3x − 10) = 90
  2. 4x − 10 = 90, so 4x = 100
  3. x = 25. The angles: 25° and 3(25) − 10 = 65°
✓ Check: 25 + 65 = 90
Watch out
  • First decide: equal or add up? Then write the equation. Setting two co-interior angles equal is the most common mistake.
  • The Z, F and C rules only work when the lines are parallel.
  • After solving for x, plug it back in: the question usually asks for the angles.
