Laser Heist: Angle Breaker

A heist game where you solve supplementary, complementary, vertical, and parallel-lines-with-a-transversal angle puzzles to crack each room's security system, then sneak the robber past roaming patrol guards through a maze to reach the exit. Play the 5-sector Campaign, endless Challenge mode, or no-pressure Practice mode. Controls: number keys to type your answer, Enter to submit, arrow keys or WASD to move the robber during a sneak.

Teaching Notes for Educators

Students solve angle relationships to break through a vault's security system, one relationship per sector.

  • The math: complementary angles (sum 90°), supplementary angles (sum 180°), and vertical angles (equal) from crossing lines.
  • Parallel lines and a transversal: corresponding and alternate angles are equal, and co-interior (same-side interior) angles add to 180°. Each puzzle names its relationship.
  • A solved angle becomes a hazard: the wedge a student calculates turns into a camera cone in a maze that they then have to sneak around.
  • Real life: camera and floodlight coverage, road intersections, and the crossing beams of bridges and ladders all depend on these angle relationships.
  • Targeted practice: Practice mode lets students choose any mix of the four relationships with no timer, and a wrong answer in the campaign gets a second try with the clock paused.
