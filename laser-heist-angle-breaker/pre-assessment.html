What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Measuring anglesEvery angle rule comes down to 90°, 180° or 360°.Turn in place: a quarter turn, a half turn, a full turn. Then read angles on a protractor together.
B: Kinds of linesParallel lines and a transversal are the heart of 8.G.A.5.Hunt for parallel and perpendicular lines in the room and on a city map.
C: Angles add up#7 and #8 are complementary and supplementary angles before they have names.Start with Bank Shot: Angle Golf, which builds exactly this.
D: EquationsThe worksheet's Parts D and E and the game's harder vaults use equations like 3x + 15 = 180.Review with Tip the Scales before the equation problems.

Reading the results: students strong in all four parts can start with vertical angles and parallel lines right away. Students who miss Part C need complementary and supplementary angles first; give them the Bank Shot printables and the visual notes. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
