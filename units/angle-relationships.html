Visual Notes: Complementary and Supplementary Angles

Grade 7 · CCSS 7.G.B.5 · Pairs with the Bank Shot: Angle Golf game
Name

The big idea: some pairs of angles always add up to the same total. If they make a corner (a right angle) they add to 90°. If they make a straight line they add to 180°. Know one angle, subtract, and you have the other. In Bank Shot, that's how you aim the ball off the walls.

Key vocabulary (the colors show the angles each word is about)

Angle

Two rays that share an endpoint (the vertex). Its size is how far one ray turns from the other.

Degree (°)

The unit for measuring angles. A full turn is 360°, so each of 8 equal pizza slices is 45°.

Right angle

Exactly 90°, a square corner. It's marked with a little box.

Straight angle

Exactly 180°. Its two sides make a straight line.

Complementary angles

Two angles that add to 90°: 35° + 55° = 90°.

Supplementary angles

Two angles that add to 180°: 70° + 110° = 180°.

Which rule do I use? (look at the picture first)

A corner (little box)?Complementary: 90 − the angle you know
A straight line?Supplementary: 180 − the angle you know
Two lines make an X?The angles across from each other (vertical angles) are equal.
Memory trick: C comes before S in the alphabet, and 90 comes before 180. Complementary makes a Corner; Supplementary makes a Straight line.

Solved Step by Step

Visual Notes: Complementary and Supplementary Angles, page 2

1. Find the complement

  1. Spot it: the little box means the two angles make 90°.
  2. Write it: x + 28 = 90
  3. Solve it: x = 90 − 28 = 62°
✓ Check: 62 + 28 = 90

2. Find the supplement

  1. Spot it: the two angles sit on a straight line, so they make 180°.
  2. Write it: x + 135 = 180
  3. Solve it: x = 180 − 135 = 45°
✓ Check: 45 + 135 = 180

3. A missing angle with an expression

  1. Straight line, so: (2x + 10) + 50 = 180
  2. Combine: 2x + 60 = 180
  3. Subtract 60: 2x = 120. Divide by 2: x = 60
  4. The angle: 2(60) + 10 = 130°
✓ Check: 130 + 50 = 180

4. Challenge: an angle is twice its complement

  1. Call the complement x. The angle is 2x.
  2. Together they make 90°: x + 2x = 90
  3. 3x = 90, so x = 30
  4. The angles: 30° and 60°
✓ Check: 60 is twice 30, and 30 + 60 = 90
Watch out
  • Complementary means 90°, supplementary means 180°. Mixing them up is the most common mistake.
  • An angle bigger than 90° has no complement. An angle of 180° or more has no supplement.
  • When the angle is an expression, solve for x, then plug x back in to find the angle.

Visual Notes: Angle Relationships

Grade 8 · CCSS 7.G.B.5, 8.G.A.5 · Pairs with the Math Billiards game
Name

The big idea: when lines cross, the angles they make are connected. Some pairs are equal, and some add up to 90° or 180°. Know one angle and you can find the others. In Math Billiards every shot is one of these problems: find x, and the ball goes where you aimed.

Key vocabulary (the colors show the angles each word is about)

Complementary angles

Two angles that add to 90°. Look for the little box: 35° + 55° = 90°.

Supplementary angles

Two angles that add to 180°. Side by side on a straight line they form a linear pair: 50° + 130° = 180°.

Vertical angles

The angles across from each other where two lines cross. They are always equal.

Transversal

A line that crosses two or more other lines (the blue line). The little arrows mean the two black lines are parallel.

Alternate interior angles

Between the parallel lines, on opposite sides of the transversal. They are equal.

Same-side interior angles

Between the parallel lines, on the same side of the transversal. They add to 180°.

Which rule do I use? (look at the picture first)

A little box?The parts add to 90°.
A straight line?The parts add to 180°.
Two lines make an X?Angles across are equal.
Parallel arrows and a Z?Alternate interior angles are equal.
An F?Corresponding angles (same spot at each crossing) are equal.
A C or U?Same-side interior angles add to 180°.

Solved Step by Step: Shots from the Game

Visual Notes: Angle Relationships, page 2

1. A shot to the cushion

  1. Spot it: the dashed guide makes a box with the cushion, so x and 40° are complementary.
  2. Write it: x + 40 = 90
  3. Solve it: x = 90 − 40 = 50°
✓ Check: 50 + 40 = 90

2. A guide line crosses the shot

  1. x is across from 62°: vertical angles, so x = 62°.
  2. y sits next to 62° on a straight line: supplementary, so y + 62 = 180.
  3. y = 180 − 62 = 118°
✓ Check: 62 + 118 = 180

3. A guide parallel to the cushion

∠2 = 70°. Find ∠6 and ∠5.

  1. ∠2 and ∠6 are in the same spot at each crossing (an F): corresponding, so ∠6 = 70°.
  2. ∠5 and ∠6 make a straight line: ∠5 = 180 − 70 = 110°.
✓ Check: ∠3 = 70° (across from ∠2), and same-side interior ∠3 + ∠5 = 180

4. Challenge: write an equation

  1. The box means the parts add to 90: x + (3x + 10) = 90
  2. Combine like terms: 4x + 10 = 90
  3. Subtract 10: 4x = 80. Divide by 4: x = 20
  4. The angles: x = 20° and 3(20) + 10 = 70°
✓ Check: 20 + 70 = 90
Watch out
  • A little box means 90°, not 180°. Subtracting from the wrong number is the most common mistake.
  • Check with your eyes: equal angles look the same size. If one is clearly bigger, the pair adds to 180° instead.
  • Only use the Z, F and C rules when the lines are parallel (look for the arrows).

Name

The big idea: when lines cross, their angles come in pairs that are either equal or add up to 90° or 180°. In the Laser Heist, you turn the laser by the angle you find. Spot the pair, decide “equal” or “adds up”, and solve.

Key vocabulary (the colors show the angles each word is about)

Complementary

Two angles that add to 90°.

Supplementary

Two angles that add to 180°.

Vertical angles

Across from each other where two lines cross. Equal.

Parallel lines

Lines in the same plane that never meet; they stay the same distance apart.

Transversal

A line that crosses two or more lines (blue).

Corresponding

Same spot at each crossing. Equal.

Alternate interior

Between the lines, opposite sides. Equal.

Co-interior

Between the lines, same side. Add to 180°.

Which rule do I use? (equal, or add up?)

A little box?Add to 90°
A straight line?Add to 180°
An X?Across: equal
A Z?Alternate interior: equal
An F?Corresponding: equal
A C or U?Co-interior: add to 180°
Shortcut for parallel lines: at two parallel lines cut by a transversal there are only two sizes of angle, a small one and a big one, and small + big = 180°. Every small angle is equal to every other small angle.

Solved Step by Step: Laser Puzzles

Visual Notes: Angle Relationships, page 2

1. Two beams cross

  1. x is across from 55°: vertical angles are equal, so x = 55°.
  2. y is next to 55° on a straight line: they add to 180°.
  3. y = 180 − 55 = 125°
✓ Check: 55 + 125 = 180

2. A beam across two parallel walls

∠2 = 72°. Find ∠6 and ∠8.

  1. ∠2 and ∠6 are corresponding (an F): equal, so ∠6 = 72°.
  2. ∠8 is a big angle (the small ones are 72°): ∠8 = 180 − 72 = 108°.
✓ Check: ∠6 + ∠8 make a straight line: 72 + 108 = 180

3. Co-interior angles with x

∠4 = (2x + 10)° and ∠6 = (x + 20)°.

  1. Between the lines, same side (a C): they add to 180°.
  2. (2x + 10) + (x + 20) = 180, so 3x + 30 = 180
  3. 3x = 150, x = 50. ∠4 = 110°, ∠6 = 70°
✓ Check: 110 + 70 = 180

4. Challenge: complementary with x

  1. The box means they add to 90°: x + (3x − 10) = 90
  2. 4x − 10 = 90, so 4x = 100
  3. x = 25. The angles: 25° and 3(25) − 10 = 65°
✓ Check: 25 + 65 = 90
Watch out
  • First decide: equal or add up? Then write the equation. Setting two co-interior angles equal is the most common mistake.
  • The Z, F and C rules only work when the lines are parallel.
  • After solving for x, plug it back in: the question usually asks for the angles.
