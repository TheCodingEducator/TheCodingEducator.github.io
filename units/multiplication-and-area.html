A rectangle with 3 rows of 4 squares has 3 × 4 = 12 squares.  ·  Not sure? You can always count each square.

Key vocabulary

  • area: the number of unit squares that cover a flat shape
  • unit square: a square with sides 1 unit long; it covers 1 square unit
  • row: a line of squares going across
  • column: a line of squares going up and down
  • array: objects arranged in equal rows and columns, like 3 rows of 4
  • factor: a number being multiplied; in 3 × 4 = 12, the factors are 3 and 4
  • product: the answer to a multiplication; in 3 × 4 = 12, the product is 12

  10. 8 (7 × 8 = 56)
  11. Any two of: 1 × 12, 2 × 6, 3 × 4 (or rotated: 12 × 1, 6 × 2, 4 × 3)
  12. Riley added the sides instead of multiplying them. 5 rows of 6 squares is 5 × 6 = 30 squares

#12 is the most common mistake: adding the sides instead of multiplying them. In the game, a wrong answer draws every square on the rectangle so students can count to check.
