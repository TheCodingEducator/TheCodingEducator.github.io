Visual Notes: Area by Multiplying

Grade 3 · CCSS 3.MD.C.7, 3.OA.C.7 · Pairs with the Area Artist game
Name

The big idea: area is how many squares cover a shape. A rectangle is made of rows that are all the same, so you don't have to count every square. Multiply the rows by the columns: 3 rows of 4 is 3 × 4 = 12 squares.

Key vocabulary

Area

How many squares cover a shape. This one: 6 squares.

Unit square

One square, 1 unit on each side. We count area in these.

Row

Squares going across, side by side.

Column

Squares going up and down.

Array

Things in equal rows and columns, like eggs in a carton.

Factor

3 × 4 = 12

The numbers you multiply: 3 and 4.

Product

3 × 4 = 12

The answer when you multiply: 12.

Three ways to find area (they all give the same answer)

1. Count1, 2, 3, … 12. Works, but slow.
2. Skip count by rows4, 8, 12. Faster!
3. Multiply the sides3 × 4 = 12. Fastest!

Solved Step by Step

Visual Notes: Area by Multiplying, page 2

1. Skip count the rows

  1. Each row has 5 squares. There are 3 rows.
  2. Skip count by 5s: 5, 10, 15
  3. Or multiply: 3 × 5 = 15 square units
✓ Same answer both ways

2. Multiply the sides

  1. The sides are 4 and 6.
  2. Area = 4 × 6 = 24 square units
✓ 6 × 4 is also 24: the order doesn't matter

3. Find the missing side

The area is 24. One side is 6. What is the other side?

  1. Think: ? × 6 = 24
  2. 4 × 6 = 24, so the missing side is 4
✓ Or divide: 24 ÷ 6 = 4

4. Break it apart

Find 4 × 7 by splitting the 7 into 5 + 2.

  1. Left part: 4 × 5 = 20
  2. Right part: 4 × 2 = 8
  3. Add: 20 + 8 = 28
✓ 4 × 7 = 28
Watch out
  • Area counts the squares inside, not the edges around the outside.
  • Count each row only once. Multiply rows by how many are in each row.
  • Write the units: square units, square inches or square feet.

Key vocabulary

  • area: the number of unit squares that cover a flat shape
  • unit square: a square with sides 1 unit long; it covers 1 square unit
  • row: a line of squares going across
  • column: a line of squares going up and down
  • array: objects arranged in equal rows and columns, like 3 rows of 4
  • factor: a number being multiplied; in 3 × 4 = 12, the factors are 3 and 4
  • product: the answer to a multiplication; in 3 × 4 = 12, the product is 12
