An ordered pair is (x, y). Start at the origin (0, 0). x comes first: move left or right. y comes second: move up or down.  ·  Negative x = left, negative y = down.

Key vocabulary

  • coordinate plane: a flat grid formed by two number lines that cross at right angles
  • x-axis: the horizontal number line
  • y-axis: the vertical number line
  • origin: the point (0, 0), where the axes cross
  • ordered pair: two numbers (x, y) that name one point; the order matters
  • x-coordinate: the first number: how far to move left (negative) or right (positive) from the origin
  • y-coordinate: the second number: how far to move down (negative) or up (positive)
  • quadrant: one of the four regions made by the axes, numbered I to IV counterclockwise from the upper right

Exit Ticket: The Coordinate Plane

Grades 5–6 · CCSS 5.G.A.1, 6.NS.C.6, 6.NS.C.8
Name
1. Which quadrant is (−2, 3) in?
2. The y-coordinate is −1 and the x-coordinate is 3. Write the ordered pair.
3. Is (0, −3) in a quadrant? Explain.

Answer Key: Battle on the Coordinate Plane

For the teacher
  1. A = (2, 5)
  2. B = (5, 2) (the same numbers as A, in the other order, make a different point)
  3. C = (0, 3) (on the y-axis)
  4. D = (4, 0) (on the x-axis)
  5. P = (−2, 3)
  6. Q = (3, −1)
  7. R = (−3, −2)
  8. S = (0, −3)
  9. E is 1 right and 2 down (Quadrant IV); F is 3 left on the x-axis; G is 1 left and 2 up (Quadrant II); H is 2 right and 3 up (Quadrant I).
  10. IV (x positive, y negative)
  11. III (both negative)
  12. axis (x = 0, so it is on the y-axis)
  13. II (x negative, y positive)
  14. axis (y = 0, so it is on the x-axis)
  15. I (both positive)
  16. (−2, 1): x always goes first, even when y is named first.
  17. (−2, −3): left 2 is x = −2, down 3 is y = −3.
  18. Sam used the second number first (and moved left for −1). x = 3 comes first: move 3 units right, then y = −1: 1 unit down. Sam's point was (−1, −3); the correct point is (3, −1).

#2, #16 and #18 catch students who reverse x and y. In the game, the plane always flies left or right first, and repeated shots are allowed, so a reversed shot lands visibly in the wrong place.

Answer Key: Exit Ticket

The Coordinate Plane
  1. Quadrant II (x negative: left; y positive: up)
  2. (3, −1) (x always goes first, even when y is named first)
  3. No. x = 0, so the point is on the y-axis, 3 units below the origin. Points on an axis are not in any quadrant.

Quick read: (−1, 3) on #2 means the student wrote the numbers in the order they were read; "Quadrant III" or "IV" on #3 means they don't yet see axis points as a separate case. Have them play Hard mode, where the computer's shots often name y first and Radar Checks include points on an axis.
