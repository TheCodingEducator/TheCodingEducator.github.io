Where this comes from: everything on this page is read directly out of your own browser's local storage for this site. It was never sent anywhere, isn't tied to your name, and nobody else - including your teacher or us - can see it. It only shows up here on this device, in this browser. Clearing your browser data (or the "Reset progress" button at the top of this page) erases it for good.
