Exponent Rules

Grades 6–8 · CCSS 6.EE.A.1, 8.EE.A.1 · Pairs with the Exponent Racer game

In 53, 5 is the base and 3 is the exponent: 53 = 5 × 5 × 5. Same base: multiply → add the exponents, divide → subtract them. Power of a power → multiply them. a0 = 1, and a−n = 1 / an.

Key vocabulary

  • base: the number being multiplied; in 4³, the base is 4
  • exponent: how many times the base is used as a factor; in 4³, the exponent is 3
  • power: a base with an exponent, like 4³
  • product of powers: same base, multiply: add the exponents (2³ · 2⁴ = 2⁷)
  • quotient of powers: same base, divide: subtract the exponents (5⁶ ÷ 5² = 5⁴)
  • power of a power: multiply the exponents ((3²)&sup4; = 3⁸)
  • zero exponent: any nonzero base to the zero power is 1 (7⁰ = 1)
  • negative exponent: means one over the positive power: 5⁻² = 1/5² = 1/25

Common mistakes to watch for: multiplying base × exponent (#18), multiplying exponents in the product rule (38 for #7), and reading a negative exponent as a negative number (−25 for #13). Exponent Racer's wrong answer choices are built from these same mistakes.
