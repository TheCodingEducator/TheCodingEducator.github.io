What's New

The games on this site are updated constantly. Here is every new game and every notable change, newest first.

September 27, 2026

  • Proportional Plates is now in Spanish. A new 🌐 Español button in the top bar switches the whole game — menus, recipes, orders, hints, customers' reactions and the "let's fix it" explanations — and the choice is remembered.
  • Spanish is rolling out to every game. Now in Spanish: Piggy Bank Math, Exponent Hoops, Exponent Racer, Linear World Cup, Let's Get to the Point, Laser Heist, Bank Shot and Similarity Builder.
  • Linear World Cup: on phones, the MENU button's "Yes, exit" and "Cancel" choices now respond to taps.
  • Linear World Cup: a goal now looks like a goal — the ball disappears into the net as it crosses the goal line.
  • Bank Shot, Exponent Hoops, Exponent Racer, Laser Heist and Let's Get to the Point: on phones, buttons near the bottom of the game screen are no longer blocked while the joystick or number pad is showing.
  • Proportional Plates: customers who are almost out of patience now complain in funny, over-the-top ways ("I've aged 10 years!", "I could eat the table!") instead of showing symbols.
  • Proportional Plates: muffins now look like real muffins — a paper liner and a golden, domed top.
  • Proportional Plates: the "let's fix it" card now shows the recipe and the order side by side joined by the scale factor (like ×4), and writes the proportion out — recipe amount over recipe size = order amount over order size — with the scale factor drawn between the tops and between the bottoms.

September 26, 2026

  • Proportional Plates: the chef can cook several orders in a row and carry two plates at once, with a trail to each table; extra plates wait on the kitchen counter to be picked up. A wrong order now goes back on the rail to be remade, and customers only storm out when their patience runs out. Level 1 uses easier numbers, the recipe pictures stay inside their boxes, and happy customers sometimes say "¡Delicioso!"
  • New: free printable worksheets and exit tickets for every game, each with an answer key. Find them on each game's teacher guide and on the For teachers page. Exit tickets print two to a page.
  • The home page and For teachers page now list grade 8 topics first (Lines and slope, Geometry), then work down to grade 2.
  • Teacher guides for every game, plus a new For teachers page that organizes the games by grade and Common Core standard.
  • Tip the Scales: fractional blocks are now drawn as a whole block with only the fraction filled in, so ¼x looks like a quarter of an x-block and the label always fits.
  • Tip the Scales: the Expand button now appears only in Variables Both Sides and Complex Equations, with a one-time pop-up that points to it and explains Expand and Factor.
  • Phones and tablets: every game page now fits on one screen with no scrolling, and the game sits clear of the on-screen joystick or number pad.
  • My Stats is organized by game and in total, and every game page has a My stats pop-out.
  • Reset progress has its own button with a 5-second countdown before anything is erased.
  • Every game now fills the screen on a computer; Standards and Teaching notes open as pop-outs.
  • Every game can be played with the keyboard alone (the mouse still works).
  • Pythagorean Platforms: a new title screen and World Map, a choice of explorer or monkey, World 5, a fraying-rope timer in Worlds 3 and 4, and no repeated triangles within a world.
  • Proportional Plates: clearer question pictures for every kind of order.

September 25, 2026

  • New game: Pythagorean Platforms, a jungle game for the Pythagorean theorem with rope-length and platform-distance swings.
  • Proportional Plates: evenly spaced tables and smoother walking.

September 24, 2026

  • New game: Proportional Plates, a diner cooking game about proportions, ratio tables and unit prices.
  • Fixed the on-screen joystick and number pad overlapping games in fullscreen on phones.
  • Bank Shot: the angle arc now spans fully from line to line.

September 22–23, 2026

  • Similarity Builder: larger dialog boxes and a more compact protester obstacle.
