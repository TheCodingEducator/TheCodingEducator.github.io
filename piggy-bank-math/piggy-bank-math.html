Piggy Bank Math

A coin-counting game where you click pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters to match a target amount before time runs out, then feed the piggy bank. Choose Penny Prospect (easy, all four coins) or Coin Captain (harder, limited coin sets and larger totals). Controls: mouse or tap to click coins and buttons, enter or space to continue.

Quick Teacher Tips

Key vocabulary

  • penny: 1 cent (1¢)
  • nickel: 5 cents (5¢)
  • dime: 10 cents (10¢)
  • quarter: 25 cents (25¢)
  • cent (¢): the smallest unit of money; 100 cents make 1 dollar
  • dollar ($): 100 cents, written $1.00

Tips for using the game

Students build a target amount from pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters, racing the clock.

  • The math: adding coin values (1, 5, 10, and 25 cents) to reach a total, which is skip counting by 5s, 10s, and 25s.
  • Two notations: totals under a dollar show as cents (75¢) and larger ones as dollars and decimals ($1.35), so students see both forms.
  • Strategy: Coin Captain limits students to two coin types at a time, which pushes them to plan a combination, and every target can be made with at least 3 coins.
  • Real life: making change, paying exact amounts, and budgeting all use this same reasoning.
  • Going over costs: a color-changing piggy bank shows how close the total is, and going over the target twice ends the round.
