1. An angle is 47°. Find its complement and its supplement.

2. The wall angle is (2x + 10)° with x = 30. Find its complement.

3. Can an angle greater than 90° have a complement? A supplement? Explain.

Answer Key: Exit Ticket

Complementary and Supplementary Angles
  1. Complement 90 − 47 = 43°; supplement 180 − 47 = 133°
  2. 2(30) + 10 = 70; 90 − 70 = 20°
  3. No complement (it's already more than 90°), but yes, a supplement, as long as it's less than 180°.
