Bank Shot: Angle Golf

A mini golf game where every hole opens with a bank-shot angle problem: the wall's known angle is shown, you solve for its complementary or supplementary partner angle, then drag from the ball to aim and set power. A correct answer sends the ball exactly where you aimed; a wrong answer deflects the shot by your own error. Real physics - hills, currents, bumpers, wall bounces - carries every shot after that. Play Golf Gamer (easy) or Hole-In-One Hero (hard) mode on one of three 9-hole courses: Medieval Kingdom (windmills), Space Station (warp portals) or Summer Beach (sliding surfboards). Best scores and stars are saved on this device.

Quick Teacher Tips

What students practice

Grade 7 · CCSS 7.G.B.5. Students solve for the missing angle in each bank shot and straight shot, then putt. The angle they type decides where the ball goes.

  • Complementary angles (add to 90°)
  • Supplementary angles (add to 180°)
  • Vertical angles (equal)
  • Simple equations such as x + 5 for an unknown angle

Printables: worksheet with answer key · exit ticket · full teacher guide

Key vocabulary

  • angle: the figure formed by two rays that share an endpoint
  • degree (°): the unit for measuring angles; a full turn is 360°
  • right angle: an angle of exactly 90°, often marked with a small square
  • straight angle: an angle of exactly 180°; its sides form a straight line
  • complementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 90° (35° and 55°)
  • supplementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 180° (110° and 70°)
  • vertical angles: the angles across from each other where two lines cross; they are always equal

Tips for using the game

Students find the missing angle in a complementary or supplementary pair, then use it to aim a mini golf shot off a wall.

  • Vertical angles: about half the straight shots are vertical-angle questions. The ball's path and a guide line cross at the ball, and the missing angle is across from the given one, so they're equal. A wrong answer sends the ball off by the difference.
  • The math: complementary angles add to 90° and supplementary angles add to 180°. A shot aimed at a wall asks for the supplementary angle (the ball's path and the wall make a straight line); a straight shot into open green asks for the complementary angle (a right angle with the aim line as one side).
  • Visible consequence: a correct answer bounces the ball like a mirror along the aimed path; a wrong one sends it off the wall at the typed angle. The student's number is drawn as a red wedge next to the known angle, with a gap if it is too small or an overflow if it is too big, and a card adds the two angles to show they miss the total.
  • Retry with hints: after a miss, a short explanation appears (without the answer), names the likely mistake (using 90° instead of 180°, typing the given angle, a subtraction slip) and the same question is asked again with a hint about the 90° or 180° total; a second miss adds the equation with a blank.
  • Algebra on the back nine: Hole-In-One Hero writes the missing angle as a tiny equation like (x + 5)

  • Real life: a ball, a billiard cue ball, a laser, or a sound wave leaves a wall at the same angle it arrived, and angle pairs like these guide roof framing, carpentry, and any layout with corners.
  • Restart a hole: the in-game Menu button can restart the current hole from the tee.
  • Built-in progression: wall angles move from multiples of 10 to multiples of 5 to any whole number, and Hole-In-One Hero shows three angles: the two equal angles of the bounce (in and out) and the one between them, so 180° − 40° − 40°.
  • Three themed courses: Medieval Kingdom, Space Station and Summer Beach, nine different holes each (a drawbridge, a black hole, a pier...). Each course has one moving obstacle: windmill sails, warp portals or sliding surfboards. Every bumper and wall is a chance for a bank shot.
  • Goals to chase: students choose a course, see their strokes on every hole and the round total, and earn up to three stars a hole. Best scores per hole and per course are saved on this device only, and a round left part-way through can be picked up again with Continue on the menu.
