Answer Key: Complementary and Supplementary Angles

For the teacher
  1. 90 − 35 = 55°
  2. 90 − 62 = 28°
  3. 10°
  4. 43°
  5. 180 − 128 = 52°
  6. 180 − 75 = 105°
  7. 20°
  8. 89°
  9. 2(25) + 10 = 60; 90 − 60 = 30°
  10. 3(20) − 5 = 55; 180 − 55 = 125°
  11. x + (x + 20) = 90, 2x = 70 → 35° and 55°
  12. x + 4x = 180, x = 36 → 36° and 144°
  13. No. Complementary angles add to 90°, and 110° is already more than 90°.
  14. Chris subtracted from 90° (the complement). Supplementary angles add to 180°: 105°

In the game, an answer that's off by 10° sends the ball 10° off course, so precision shows up right on the green. Hole-In-One Hero mode uses expressions like the ones in Part C.
