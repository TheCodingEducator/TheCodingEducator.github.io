Visual Notes: Complementary and Supplementary Angles

Grade 7 · CCSS 7.G.B.5 · Pairs with the Bank Shot: Angle Golf game
Name

The big idea: some pairs of angles always add up to the same total. If they make a corner (a right angle) they add to 90°. If they make a straight line they add to 180°. Know one angle, subtract, and you have the other. In Bank Shot, that's how you aim the ball off the walls.

Key vocabulary (the colors show the angles each word is about)

Angle

Two rays that share an endpoint (the vertex). Its size is how far one ray turns from the other.

Degree (°)

The unit for measuring angles. A full turn is 360°, so each of 8 equal pizza slices is 45°.

Right angle

Exactly 90°, a square corner. It's marked with a little box.

Straight angle

Exactly 180°. Its two sides make a straight line.

Complementary angles

Two angles that add to 90°: 35° + 55° = 90°.

Supplementary angles

Two angles that add to 180°: 70° + 110° = 180°.

Which rule do I use? (look at the picture first)

A corner (little box)?Complementary: 90 − the angle you know
A straight line?Supplementary: 180 − the angle you know
Two lines make an X?The angles across from each other (vertical angles) are equal.
Memory trick: C comes before S in the alphabet, and 90 comes before 180. Complementary makes a Corner; Supplementary makes a Straight line.

Solved Step by Step

Visual Notes: Complementary and Supplementary Angles, page 2

1. Find the complement

  1. Spot it: the little box means the two angles make 90°.
  2. Write it: x + 28 = 90
  3. Solve it: x = 90 − 28 = 62°
✓ Check: 62 + 28 = 90

2. Find the supplement

  1. Spot it: the two angles sit on a straight line, so they make 180°.
  2. Write it: x + 135 = 180
  3. Solve it: x = 180 − 135 = 45°
✓ Check: 45 + 135 = 180

3. A missing angle with an expression

  1. Straight line, so: (2x + 10) + 50 = 180
  2. Combine: 2x + 60 = 180
  3. Subtract 60: 2x = 120. Divide by 2: x = 60
  4. The angle: 2(60) + 10 = 130°
✓ Check: 130 + 50 = 180

4. Challenge: an angle is twice its complement

  1. Call the complement x. The angle is 2x.
  2. Together they make 90°: x + 2x = 90
  3. 3x = 90, so x = 30
  4. The angles: 30° and 60°
✓ Check: 60 is twice 30, and 30 + 60 = 90
Watch out
  • Complementary means 90°, supplementary means 180°. Mixing them up is the most common mistake.
  • An angle bigger than 90° has no complement. An angle of 180° or more has no supplement.
  • When the angle is an expression, solve for x, then plug x back in to find the angle.
