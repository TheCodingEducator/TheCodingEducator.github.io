What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Measuring anglesComplementary and supplementary are defined by 90° and 180°.Use a clock and a pizza: quarter turn = 90°, half turn = 180°, full turn = 360°.
B: ProtractorStudents check their answers by measuring, and the game's angles are drawn to scale.Practice which scale to read: the one that starts at 0 on the angle's first side.
C: Angles add up#7 and #8 are complementary and supplementary angles before they have names.Fold paper: a corner is 90°, a fold line is 180°. Cut it into two angles and measure.
D: EquationsEvery missing angle is an equation like x + 35 = 90; the game's Hole-In-One Hero mode uses them.Review with Tip the Scales (one-step equations).

Reading the results: students who get #7, #8 and #11 right already understand the idea; they only need the words complementary and supplementary, and the visual notes give them those. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
