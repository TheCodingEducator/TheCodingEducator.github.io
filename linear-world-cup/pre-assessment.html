  9. 150 ÷ 3 = 50 mph

What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: PointsEvery pass in the game goes from (0, b) to a point students must read.Play Battle on the Coordinate Plane, or practice "x first: walk, then climb."
B: Negative numbersSlope = (y2 − y1) ÷ (x2 − x1) uses exactly these steps.A number-line warm-up on subtracting negatives before Part A of the worksheet.
C: RatesSlope is a rate: rise per 1 run. #8 and #10 are proportional lines, y = mx.Review unit rates with Proportional Plates.
D: Tables and rules#11 is already y = mx + b: $5 per visit is the slope and $10 is the y-intercept.Build the table together and look for the "+5 each time" pattern before naming slope.

Reading the results: students who get #7, #8 and #11 already think in rates; connect "rise over run" and "$5 per visit" to slope on day one. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
