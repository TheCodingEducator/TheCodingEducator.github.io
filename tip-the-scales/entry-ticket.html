A warm-up for the start of class, before students play or start the worksheet. It reviews missing numbers and expressions using things students see every day, then asks them to think ahead to a two-step equation. Two tickets print on each page; cut along the dashed line. The answer key prints on its own page after it.

Answer Key: Entry Ticket

Keep It Balanced
  1. x = 9
  2. 12 ÷ 3 = $4
  3. 2(5) + 3 = 13
  4. x = 4. Most students take away the 3 first (11 − 3 = 8), then split 8 into 2 equal groups. That's exactly today's two steps: undo the adding, then undo the multiplying.

Quick read: 16 on #3 means the student added before multiplying (2 × 8). Students who solve #4 by guess-and-check are ready to learn the inverse-operation steps; ask them to explain their guesses.
