What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Missing numbersOne-step equations are missing-number problems with a letter.Start with the balance picture: what keeps both sides equal?
B: Order of operationsSolving undoes the order of operations in reverse; checking uses it forward.A quick review of multiply-before-add with a few warm-ups.
C: ExpressionsParts C and D of the worksheet need the distributive property and combining like terms.Area models for 3(x + 2) and algebra tiles for combining terms.
D: SolutionsEvery answer should be checked by substituting it back in.Model "plug it in" with #9 before the first lesson.
E: IntegersMany equations have negative numbers or negative answers.A number-line warm-up on adding and subtracting negatives.

Reading the results: students strong in Parts A, B and D can start with one- and two-step equations right away; Part C decides who is ready for Part D of the worksheet (the distributive property). Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
