Visual Notes: The Pythagorean Theorem

Grade 8 · CCSS 8.G.B.6, 8.G.B.7 · Pairs with the Pythagorean Platforms game
Name

The big idea: in any right triangle, the squares on the two short sides together have the same area as the square on the long side: a² + b² = c². In the picture, 9 + 16 = 25. Know two sides and you can always find the third.

Key vocabulary

Right triangle

A triangle with one right angle (90°), marked with a little box.

Legs (a and b)

The two sides that make the right angle.

Hypotenuse (c)

The longest side, across from the right angle.

Pythagorean theorem

a² + b² = c²

True for every right triangle, and only for right triangles.

Square root (√)

The number that times itself gives the original: √25 = 5, because 5 × 5 = 25.

Perfect squares

1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144, 169, 196, 225

Square roots of these are whole numbers. Famous triangles: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.

Which way do I solve?

Missing the hypotenuse?Add the squares, then take √: c = √(a² + b²)
Missing a leg?Subtract the squares, then take √: a = √(c² − b²)
Is it a right triangle?Check if the two smaller sides squared add up to the biggest squared.

Solved Step by Step

Visual Notes: The Pythagorean Theorem, page 2

1. Find the hypotenuse (the rope)

  1. Plug in: 7² + 24² = c²
  2. Square: 49 + 576 = c²
  3. Add: 625 = c²
  4. Square root: c = √625 = 25
✓ Check: 25 is longer than both legs

2. Find a missing leg (the gap)

  1. Plug in: a² + 10² = 26²
  2. Square: a² + 100 = 676
  3. Subtract: a² = 676 − 100 = 576
  4. Square root: a = √576 = 24
✓ Check: 24 is shorter than the hypotenuse, 26

3. Real life: what size TV?

A TV screen is 40 in wide and 30 in tall. TVs are sold by the diagonal.

  1. The width, height and diagonal make a right triangle.
  2. 30² + 40² = 900 + 1600 = 2500
  3. Diagonal = √2500 = 50 in: a “50-inch TV”
✓ Check: it's the 3-4-5 triangle, times 10

4. Is it a right triangle?

Sides 9, 12 and 15.

  1. The longest side would be c: c = 15.
  2. 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225
  3. 15² = 225. They match, so yes, it's a right triangle.
✓ If they don't match (like 6, 7, 9: 36 + 49 = 85, not 81), it isn't.
Watch out
  • Square the sides before adding: 6 + 8 = 14 is wrong; 6² + 8² = 100, so c = 10.
  • 4² means 4 × 4 = 16, not 4 × 2.
  • The hypotenuse goes by itself on one side of the equation. Finding a leg means subtracting.
