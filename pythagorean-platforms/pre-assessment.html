What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Squaresa² + b² = c² is squaring three times. 4² = 8 is the most common slip in the unit.Build squares from tiles: a 4-by-4 square has 16 tiles, not 8.
B: Square rootsThe last step of every problem is a square root.Play Rooted to the Spot and post the perfect squares to 625.
C: Right trianglesThe theorem only works for right triangles; the hypotenuse is across from the right angle.Find right angles in the room (book corners, door frames) and the side across from each.
D: EquationsFinding a missing leg means solving equations like 81 + b² = 225.Review with Tip the Scales.

Reading the results: students strong in Parts A and B are ready to find hypotenuses on day one; give students who miss Part B a perfect-squares chart for the first few days. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
