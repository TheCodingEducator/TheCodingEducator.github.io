What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: PointsEvery transformation is done point by point on the grid.Play Battle on the Coordinate Plane first.
B: OppositesReflections over an axis just change one coordinate to its opposite. #4 and #5 are reflections already.Fold a coordinate grid along each axis and see which points land on each other.
C: IntegersTranslations add to the coordinates: (x + 3, y − 5).A number-line warm-up: "start at −3, move 5 right."
D: Symmetry and turnsReflections are folds; rotations are turns of 90°, 180° or 270°, clockwise or counterclockwise.Turn a sheet of paper a quarter turn at a time on the desk, with a dot to follow.

Reading the results: students who get #4 and #5 already understand reflections over the axes; move them quickly to rotations. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
