Answer Key: Exit Ticket

Transformations
  1. (−1 + 5, 3 − 4) → (4, −1)
  2. (−y, x) → (3, 2)
  3. (−4, −5). Only y changes: the point flips to the other side of the x-axis but stays the same distance left of the y-axis.

Quick read: (−3, −2) on #2 is a clockwise rotation (wrong direction); (4, 5) on #3 is a reflection over the wrong axis. Both are named mistakes in the game's Practice mode.
