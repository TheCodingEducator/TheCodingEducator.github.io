Overview

TheCodingEducator's Games ("this site") offers free browser-based math games for students and classrooms. This page explains what information is - and isn't - collected when you visit or play.

We do not collect personal information

This site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no logins, and no forms. The games do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and none is stored by this site.

Advertising

This site is supported by Google AdSense. Because this site is intended for a young audience, it has been designated with Google as directed to children. This means:

  • No personalized or interest-based advertising is shown.
  • No remarketing or behavioral ad targeting is used.
  • Ads are served without building a profile of you or your browsing history.

Google may still process limited technical information (such as general location or browser/device type) to deliver ads and to detect invalid traffic, in line with its own policies. You can read more in Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub Pages may process standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) as part of normal web server operation. This is not used by this site to identify individual visitors. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

This site is directed, in part, to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), advertising on this site is configured to disable personalized and behavioral ad targeting for all visitors.

No data to request or delete

Because this site does not collect or store personal information directly, there is generally nothing held about an individual visitor to access, correct, or delete. If you have a question or concern about this - as a parent, guardian, or educator - please contact us using the information below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact us

Questions or concerns about this policy or the site can be sent to: thecodingeducatorgames@gmail.com, or via our Contact page.
