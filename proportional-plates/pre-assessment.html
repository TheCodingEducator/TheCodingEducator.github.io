What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: FractionsFractional scale factors (× ½, × 1½) are Part D of the worksheet and the game's harder orders.Fraction strips or a quick review of multiplying a whole number by a fraction.
B: MoneyUnit prices divide money by a count.Practice with play money: share $4.50 among 3 people.
C: RatiosA recipe is a ratio; scaling it keeps the ratio the same.Build ratio tables with counters before the game.
D: Unit ratesThe unit rate is the constant of proportionality, k in y = kx.Grocery flyers: find the price of 1 for several deals.

Reading the results: students strong in Parts C and D can start scaling recipes on day one; save fractional scale factors until Part A is solid. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
