Exit Ticket: Square Roots and Cube Roots

Grade 8 · CCSS 8.EE.A.2, 8.NS.A.2
Name
1. A square has an area of 121 square units. How long is each side?
2. √60 is between which two whole numbers?
and
3. ∛64 = ?

Answer Key: Exit Ticket

Square Roots and Cube Roots
  1. 11 (11 × 11 = 121)
  2. 7 and 8 (49 < 60 < 64)
  3. 4 (4 × 4 × 4 = 64)

Quick read: 60.5 on #1 means the student halved the area; 21 on #3 means they divided by 3. Have them play with just Square Roots and Side of a Square turned on, where each wrong answer draws the square, then add Cube Roots.
