We'd love to hear from you

If you're a parent, guardian, or teacher and you've run into a problem with one of the games, have a question about how they work, or just want to share feedback, please reach out. Every message is read.

thecodingeducatorgames@gmail.com

What to include

To help us sort things out quickly, it's helpful to mention:

  • Which game you're contacting us about (e.g. Exponent Racer, Exponent Hoops)
  • What happened, or what your question is
  • What device and browser you were using, if it's a technical issue

Common reasons to reach out

  • A game isn't loading, freezes, or behaves unexpectedly
  • A question or math problem seems incorrect
  • Feedback on how a game is (or isn't) working well in a classroom
  • Privacy or COPPA-related questions - see our Privacy Policy for more detail
  • Anything else on your mind
