Complementary angles add to 90°. Supplementary angles add to 180°. Vertical angles (across from each other) are equal.
Parallel lines cut by a transversal: corresponding, alternate interior and alternate exterior angles are equal; same-side interior angles add to 180°.

Key vocabulary

  • complementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 90°
  • supplementary angles: two angles whose measures add to 180°; side by side on a line they form a linear pair
  • vertical angles: the angles across from each other where two lines cross; they are equal
  • transversal: a line that crosses two or more other lines
  • alternate interior angles: between the parallel lines, on opposite sides of the transversal; equal
  • same-side interior angles: between the parallel lines, on the same side of the transversal; they add to 180°

Answer Key: Angle Relationships: Pool Shots

For the teacher
  1. 90 − 55 = 35°
  2. 90 − 17 = 73°
  3. 180 − 145 = 35°
  4. 180 − 38 = 142°
  5. x is vertical to 47° → x = 47°; y is adjacent (supplementary) → y = 133°
  6. x = 128°; y = 52°
  7. ∠1 = 116°, ∠3 = 64°, ∠4 = 116°, ∠5 = 116°, ∠6 = 64°, ∠7 = 64°, ∠8 = 116°
  8. a) corresponding (equal)   b) alternate interior (equal)   c) same-side interior (add to 180°)   d) alternate exterior (equal)
  9. The cushion and the perpendicular make a right angle: x + 32 = 90, x = 58°
  10. A straight line: x + 121 = 180, x = 59°
  11. Vertical angles are equal: x = 47°
  12. Alternate interior: x = 64°. y and x are same-side interior angles: 180 − 64, y = 116°
  13. x + 2x + 15 = 90, 3x = 75 → x = 25; angles 25° and 65°
  14. Jamal subtracted from 180°, but the box means the parts add to 90°: x = 28°. (In the game, 118° really points the ball 90° away from the shot he aimed.)

In the game, a wrong answer plays the shot that angle really makes, then shows both paths. Problem 14 is a good one to act out: draw Jamal's 118° from the same guide line and see where it points.
