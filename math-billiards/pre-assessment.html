Answer Key: Pre-Assessment

Getting Ready for Angle Relationships · for the teacher
  1. 90°, 180°, 360°
  2. a) obtuse (125°)   b) acute (40°)   c) straight (180°)   d) right (90°)
  3. a) perpendicular   b) crossing   c) parallel
  4. No, they never meet: they are parallel. (A student who adds that the avenue crosses both already has the idea of a transversal.)
  5. 35 + 50 = 85°
  6. 110 − 40 = 70°
  7. 90 − 28 = 62° (complementary)
  8. 180 − 115 = 65° (supplementary)
  9. 38°: the blades and the handles are vertical angles, so they are equal.
  10. 90 − 64 = 26°: the wall and the ground make a right angle, so the ladder's two angles are complementary.
  11. x = 43
  12. 3x = 150 → x = 50
  13. x + x + 40 = 180, 2x = 140 → x = 70; the angles are 70° and 110°

What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Angle basicsEvery rule in the unit comes down to 90°, 180° or 360°.A 5-minute warm-up: show a right angle, a straight angle and a full turn with arms or a paper plate.
B: Kinds of linesThe new standard is all about parallel lines and a line that crosses them.Find parallel and perpendicular lines in the room: door frames, floor tiles, notebook lines.
C: Angles add upEach angle-pair problem splits or combines angles.Fold or cut a paper angle into two parts and measure the parts and the whole.
D: Angle pairsThe direct foundation: parallel-line angles are found with these same pairs.Play Bank Shot: Angle Golf, or Math Billiards Practice mode with only Complementary, Supplementary and Vertical on.
E: EquationsThe worksheet's Part E and the game's Challenge level use two-step equations.Review with Tip the Scales before the Challenge problems.

Reading the results: students strong in Parts A–D are ready to start with parallel lines right away. A student who misses Part D but not A–C usually knows the facts but not the names; the visual notes fix that quickly. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
