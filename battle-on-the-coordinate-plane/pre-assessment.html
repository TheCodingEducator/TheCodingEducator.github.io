What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Number linesThe x-axis and y-axis are two number lines.Walk a number line taped on the floor before graphing.
B: LinesThe axes are perpendicular number lines that cross at 0.Draw a plus sign and label the center 0.
C: Finding a spotAn ordered pair is like a row and seat: two numbers in a fixed order.Seat students by “row, seat” tickets, then switch to (x, y).
D: Below zeroGrade 6 uses all four quadrants, with negative coordinates.Thermometers and elevators with basements; play the first-quadrant levels of the game first.

Reading the results: grade 5 students only need Parts A to C to start (first quadrant); Part D tells grade 6 teachers who is ready for all four quadrants. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who circles “I've got it” but misses the part is worth a quick conversation.
