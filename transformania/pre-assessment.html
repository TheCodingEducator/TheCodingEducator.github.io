Answer Key: Pre-Assessment

Getting Ready for Transformations
  1. 4 units, to the right. Pick one square and count to the matching square.
  2. 3 units, down.
  3. A vertical line down the middle of the body; the two halves match exactly (one wing lands on the other).
  4. Shade the mirror squares on the right of the line: each square is the same distance from the line, on the other side.
  5. quarter 90°, half 180°, three-quarter 270°, full 360°
  6. clockwise (from the top toward the right)
  7. quarter turn: right; half turn: down
  8. The figure on the left of the yellow ones (it is the blue figure turned): same squares, same arrangement. The one on the right has 5 squares, not 4.

What each part tells you

PartWhy it matters for this unitIf many students miss it
A: Counting unitsTranslations are counted square by square, in a direction.Practice in Transformania with only Translations on.
B: SymmetryA reflection is a fold: each square lands the same distance from the line, on the other side.Fold paper shapes along a line before playing.
C: TurnsRotations are counted in quarter-turns of 90°, clockwise or counterclockwise.Turn real tracing paper around a pencil point, one quarter-turn at a time.
D: Same size and shapeEvery image is congruent to its pre-image.Cut out a shape and move it on the grid.

Reading the results: students strong on Parts A and C can start with the computer on Normal; students who missed Part C should do the guided tutorial and Practice with only Rotations first. Compare the “How sure do you feel?” circles with the answers: a student who feels sure but missed it needs a quick conversation.
