Answer Key: Entry Ticket

Slide, Flip, Turn
  1. 2 × 90° = 180°
  2. A vertical line down the middle of the butterfly's body.
  3. Every square moves 3 units right; the shape doesn’t turn.

Quick read: 90° on #1 means a student counts one turn instead of two; a figure that turned on #3 mixes up sliding and turning. Use #3 to open the lesson, then play Transformania.
