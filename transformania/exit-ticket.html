Answer Key: Exit Ticket

Transform the Figure

pre-image image

1. Every square moves 4 units left.
2. 2 quarter-turns (180° ÷ 90°), clockwise around the red point.
3. Each square lands the same distance below the line as it was above it.

Quick read: an image that moved 4 units right on #1 is a direction mix-up; on #2, an answer of 1 or 3 turns means the student is counting 180° as one turn or turning past it.
